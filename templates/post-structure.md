# Post structure

Every post follows this structure. The reviewer agent rejects posts that don't.

## 1. Title
- Matches the target search or question closely.
- Under 65 characters where possible.

## 2. The answer (first paragraph)
- Answers the title's question directly in the first two or three sentences.
- No warm-up, no "In today's world."
- Search engines and AI assistants pull answers from the top of the page, so this is the part most likely to be quoted.

## 3. TL;DR
- Right after the answer.
- One to three short lines, labeled **TL;DR**.

## 4. Body
- Headings phrased the way buyers ask questions.
- Short paragraphs.
- Every claim with a number or a fact links to its source.

## 5. Action steps (when the topic has a process)
- A numbered list the reader can follow.
- Each step starts with a verb.
- Skip this section if the topic has no real steps. Don't invent them.

## 6. FAQ
- Three to six questions buyers actually ask about this topic.
- Each answer is two to four sentences and fully stands on its own.
- Marked up with FAQPage schema in the HTML (see `post.html`).

## 7. Call to action
- Points to the matching offer in `context/offers.md`.

## Publishing card (delivered alongside the post)
- Title
- Meta description (under 155 characters)
- URL slug
- Suggested category
- Alt text for any images
- Target keyword
